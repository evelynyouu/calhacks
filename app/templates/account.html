How partial shares work

  • You buy by the dollar, not the share. $25 of a $350 stock is about 0.07 of a share; you get the same percent gain or loss as a whole share.
  • Dividends are paid in proportion: own 0.07 of a share, get 0.07 of each dividend.
  • Real brokerages (Alpaca included) fill dollar orders as market orders during trading hours, and not every stock allows partial shares. An order placed on a weekend waits for Monday.
  • Partial shares usually can't move to another brokerage. Switching means selling them first, which can create a taxable gain.
  • Voting rights for partial shares vary by brokerage; often they're combined or not passed through.

Data & privacy

Your sign-in account (email, password hash, 2FA) is stored in the server's user database. Your answers, chat and simulated trades live in this server's memory and disappear when it restarts. Claude sees your answers and fund numbers to write replies; it never sees bank or brokerage keys.

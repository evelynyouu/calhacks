How partial shares work

  • Buy by the dollar: $25 of a $350 stock is 0.07 shares, with the same gain or loss per share as a whole one.
  • Dividends paid in proportion to your fraction.
  • Not every stock allows partials; weekend orders wait for Monday.
  • Usually can't transfer to another brokerage: you'd sell first, which can create a taxable gain.

Data & privacy

Answers, chat and trades are in-memory and reset on restart. Claude sees your fund numbers but never bank or brokerage keys. FirstFund is a guide, not a registered adviser.
